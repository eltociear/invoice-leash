Invoice Leash

An invoice-paying AI agent that stays safe when its model does not. The agent's code has no limit check, no vendor check and no signature check; invoices are in fUSD and signed with ML-DSA-87. Every limit lives on QoreChain, so we can hand the agent to an attacker and watch the chain say no.

An agent that can move nothing

The agent acts as its own account, which holds no fUSD. Its QoreChain rule lists no QOR and no fUSD, and allows one call: InvoiceDeskV2.pay. It cannot send, transfer, approve or reconfigure.

The company's money, the company's limits

fUSD stays in the company's account (no vault). The desk pulls it per invoice, within 100 fUSD a payment and 250 a day that only the company can set.

Post-quantum invoices

ML-DSA-87 signature from an approved vendor whose key is registered on chain, over payer, payee, currency and amount, checked by precompiles 0x…0a02 and 0x…0a01.

The inbox, email by email (v2, fUSD)

Recorded on qorechain-diana. Two honest vendors, Hostline and Tokenwell, and Mallory, who holds a valid ML-DSA-87 key of her own but is not a vendor. The compromised agent obeys every email, attacker's included, and tries every way to move money.

What we found: a spending rule bounds amounts, not destinations

v1 let the agent act as the company's account, scoped to pay by a QoreChain spending rule. These probes, each one request by the agent's key, showed the key could still move value directly in any denom its rule allows (a plain QOR transfer, or fUSD.transfer), and could not pay in an unlisted token at all. v2 gives the agent an account that holds nothing.
